Fox Recovery
Appointment Policy

At Fox Recovery, our mission is to deliver high‑quality care and support to every client in a timely and respectful manner. Missed appointments, late arrivals, and short‑notice cancellations reduce access for others and disrupt the smooth running of our services.

Booking and Cancellations

    Notification Requirements:
  • Contact Pip by telephone (07874 243 985) or email pip@foxrecoveryuk.com to cancel or reschedule. Alternatively please cancel directly via the Jane App booking system.
    • Advance Notice:
  • Cancellations must be made at least 24 hours prior to the scheduled appointment. This allows us to offer the time to another client.

Late Cancellations and No‑Shows

  • A late cancellation is defined as cancelling less than 24 hours before the appointment.
  • A no‑show occurs when a patient does not attend and fails to cancel in advance.
  • In either case, the full appointment fee will be charged.

Providing timely notice of cancellation helps us maintain access to care for all patients and supports the smooth running of our clinic.

Privacy Notice

All orders provided through our website, or bookings via JaneApp, are processed through Stripe (our payment processing system). Stripe is a highly trusted online card processor of both debit and credit cards and is compliant with Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard Version 4.0 – currently the highest level of compliance. We never store any of your bank details on any of our systems.

Information We Collect

We may collect, store or use any of the following information in order to provide services to you:

  • Name, address, contact information (telephone/e-mail address)
  • Details of previous orders, bookings or transactions.
  • Information that you provide to us for the purposes of registering with us.
  • Information you provide to us in order to subscribe to our mailing list.
  • Information about your computer and website visit – this may include your IP address, length of visit and number of page views – none of which can be used to identify an individual user.
  • Any other information you chose to send us.
  • Information you may provide us regarding the type of treatment you request.

    • Provision of this information allows us to contact you with any relevant information in relation to your booking or order – for example, if we need to reschedule a booking, cancellations or refunds.
